# 03 · Opportunity Tree

**Stage:** Analyze · **Updates:** `goals, opportunities`

## Purpose

Connect one measurable goal to the customer opportunities that could move it, so solutions are judged against the goal, not against each other.

## Inputs

- Needs (N#)
- Business objective

## Steps

1. Write the goal (`G#`) as a measurable outcome and link it to a KPI (create the north star KPI now if needed).
2. List 3–6 opportunities (`O#`): customer problems behind the needs, phrased as problems, not solutions. Link each to its needs and the goal.
3. Drop opportunities that do not plausibly move the goal; note why.
4. Mark the 1–3 opportunities to pursue first and why (size of need, evidence strength, fit).

## Check before moving on

- [ ] At least 3 opportunities.
- [ ] No solutions at the opportunity level.
- [ ] Each opportunity links needs and the goal.
- [ ] `python scripts/canvas_check.py canvas-output/canvas.json` has no errors

## Common mistake

- A tree with one branch is a disguised solution.
