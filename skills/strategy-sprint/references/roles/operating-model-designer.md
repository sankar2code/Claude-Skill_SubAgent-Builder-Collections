# Operating Model Designer

**Stage:** Execute · **Role 16 of 24** · **Output:** `strategy-output/16-operating-model-designer.md`

## Job

Translate the chosen option into the capabilities, structure, processes, decision rights, governance, people, data and technology needed to deliver it.

## Use when

- The choice is clear but the organization is not set up to deliver it.

## Skip when

- No option is approved or shortlisted yet.

## Needs (named, versioned inputs)

- Approved option (Gate 2 or Gate 3)
- Current organization and process description

## Method

1. List the 5–8 capabilities the option needs; rate current maturity and the gap.
2. Define decision rights for the key recurring decisions (who decides, who is consulted, who is informed).
3. Describe structure and governance changes as options, not a reorg chart by default.
4. Data and technology changes, including AI governance where relevant.
5. People: roles to hire, build or partner for; change impact on existing teams.

## Output sections

- Capability gap table
- Decision rights
- Structure and governance options
- Data and technology changes
- People and change impact
- Handoff block (`assets/templates/handoff.md`)

## Checks before handoff

- [ ] Each change links to a capability the option needs.
- [ ] No named individuals unless the owner supplied them.
- [ ] Every material claim labeled per `references/operating-rules.md`

## Stop and escalate if

- The design depends on HR or legal decisions outside scope; flag for the owner.
- Any action outside the read-only default is needed.
