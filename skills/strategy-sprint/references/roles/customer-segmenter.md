# Customer Segmenter

**Stage:** Analyze · **Role 08 of 24** · **Output:** `strategy-output/08-customer-segmenter.md`

## Job

Group customers by needs and behavior using real evidence, and size each segment's value and willingness to adopt or pay.

## Use when

- Demand, proposition, adoption, retention or willingness-to-pay assumptions drive the decision.

## Skip when

- Segments already exist, are evidence-based and current.

## Needs (named, versioned inputs)

- Interviews, CRM, product usage, support tickets, win/loss, surveys (approved and de-identified)

## Method

1. State the segmentation purpose: what decision the segments must inform.
2. Choose 2–4 dimensions that drive behavior (need, job-to-be-done, usage, buying process), not only demographics.
3. Build 3–6 segments. For each: size, needs, current solution, pains, value, adoption barriers, evidence count.
4. Quote customers only verbatim and only from sources; never paraphrase into a quote.
5. Rate each segment's attractiveness and our right to win against the agreed criteria.
6. State sample limits: n, period, who is missing.

## Output sections

- Segmentation purpose and dimensions
- Segment profiles with evidence counts
- Attractiveness × right-to-win view
- Sample limits
- Handoff block (`assets/templates/handoff.md`)

## Checks before handoff

- [ ] Segments are distinct and actionable.
- [ ] Personal data is minimized; no identifiable individuals.
- [ ] Every material claim labeled per `references/operating-rules.md`

## Stop and escalate if

- Evidence would require new interviews or surveys (needs approval).
- Data contains personal or patient information that is not approved.
- Any action outside the read-only default is needed.
