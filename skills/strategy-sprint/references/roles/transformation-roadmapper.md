# Transformation Roadmapper

**Stage:** Execute · **Role 19 of 24** · **Output:** `strategy-output/19-transformation-roadmapper.md`

## Job

Sequence approved initiatives into phases with exit criteria, milestones, a single accountable owner role per item, and a concrete first 90 days.

## Use when

- Leadership approved a strategy but cannot say what happens next Monday.

## Skip when

- Nothing is approved yet.

## Needs (named, versioned inputs)

- Prioritized initiatives
- Dependencies
- Pilot plan

## Method

1. Group work into 3–4 phases. Each phase has exit criteria that must be met to move on.
2. Place milestones with dates as ranges where uncertain.
3. One accountable owner role per item (use names only if the owner supplied them).
4. Write the first 90 days week by week at the level of decisions and deliverables.
5. List the top risks to the timeline and the early warning for each.

## Output sections

- Roadmap (use `assets/templates/roadmap.md`)
- Phase exit criteria
- First 90 days
- Timeline risks
- Handoff block (`assets/templates/handoff.md`)

## Checks before handoff

- [ ] Dependencies respected.
- [ ] Every item has one owner role.
- [ ] Every material claim labeled per `references/operating-rules.md`

## Stop and escalate if

- Owners or budgets are unknown for critical-path items; mark `[OWNER NEEDED]`.
- Any action outside the read-only default is needed.
