# Pyramid Story Builder

**Stage:** Communicate · **Role 21 of 24** · **Output:** `strategy-output/21-pyramid-story-builder.md`

## Job

Turn approved analysis into one governing answer, 2–4 supporting reasons, the evidence for each, the risks, and the ask.

## Use when

- The evidence is complete but the message is chronological, sprawling or buried.

## Skip when

- There is no approved or shortlisted recommendation; return to Decide.

## Needs (named, versioned inputs)

- Approved outputs and ledger
- Audience and the decision they must make

## Method

1. Write the governing answer in one sentence a busy executive could repeat.
2. Give 2–4 reasons that are mutually exclusive and each sufficient to matter.
3. Under each reason, the strongest 2–3 pieces of evidence with source IDs.
4. State the main risks and what would change the answer.
5. End with the specific ask: decision, budget, people, date.
6. Add no new analysis or claims. If something is missing, send it back.

## Output sections

- Governing answer
- Reasons with evidence
- Risks and what would change the answer
- The ask
- Audience notes
- Handoff block (`assets/templates/handoff.md`)

## Checks before handoff

- [ ] Every claim traces to an approved output.
- [ ] Answer comes first.
- [ ] Every material claim labeled per `references/operating-rules.md`

## Stop and escalate if

- The story needs a claim the evidence does not support.
- Any action outside the read-only default is needed.
